FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a typical project take?

Starter websites: 1–2 weeks. Custom portals/apps: 4–6 weeks including design, build, and testing. We give you a timeline upfront and stick to it.

Do I own the code and design?

Yes, 100%. You get the full source code, design files, and we deploy to your accounts (GitHub, Netlify, domain). No lock-in.

What’s your support like after launch?

Every project includes 30 days bug-fix support. After that, optional care plans from $199/month for updates, backups, and small changes.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Absolutely. We integrate with Stripe, Xero, Airtable, CRMs, and custom APIs. Tell us your stack and we’ll scope it.

Do you offer hosting?

We deploy to Netlify/Vercel/AWS for speed and reliability, or to your preferred host. You own the accounts — we just make it seamless.

How do payments work?

50% deposit to start, 50% on launch. Fixed-price quote after discovery — no hourly surprises. Invoices in AUD.
