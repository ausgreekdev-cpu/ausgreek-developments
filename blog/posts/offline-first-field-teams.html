Offline-first field teams: why connectivity can't be assumed

Construction, mining, and logistics teams work in places where the internet doesn't. Designing for offline isn't a nice-to-have — it's the difference between a tool that gets used and one that gets abandoned.

The connectivity reality

Remote sites, basements, warehouses, and rural areas have patchy or no signal. Workers can't wait for a spinner. If the app doesn't work offline, they'll go back to paper.

Principles we follow

  • Local-first writes: every action saves locally first, syncs later.
  • No blocking: never make the user wait on a network request.
  • Conflict resolution: handle concurrent edits gracefully.
  • Sync status: show what's synced and what's pending.
  • Graceful degradation: read-only mode when fully offline.

The payoff

Field teams capture data without waiting on a signal. Office gets real-time visibility when connected. Zero lost dockets, zero double entry.
