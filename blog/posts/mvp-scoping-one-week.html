How we scope an MVP in one week

Most projects fail because the scope was wrong, not because the code was bad. Here's our 5-day process for turning a vague idea into a fixed-price plan.

Day 1: The brain dump

We sit down (or jump on a call) and you tell us everything. Features, users, workflows, integrations, dreams. No filtering. We write it all down.

Day 2: The cut

We identify the single most important user journey. Everything else is v2. We cut features ruthlessly. The question isn't "what's nice to have" — it's "what proves value in 4 weeks?"

Day 3: The scope doc

One page. What we're building, what we're not, timeline, and fixed price. You review, we adjust, you approve.

Day 4: The prototype

A clickable wireframe of the core flow. You click through it, we iterate. Changes here cost minutes.

Day 5: The plan

Final scope, fixed price, 50/50 payment terms, start date. You sign, we start building.

Why this works

  • No scope creep: we agree on "done" before code.
  • No hourly surprises: fixed price, fixed timeline.
  • No wasted work: we validate the idea before building it.
