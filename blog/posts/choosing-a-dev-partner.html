How to choose a software development partner

Price and portfolio matter, but they're not enough. Here's a practical checklist for choosing a partner who'll actually deliver.

1. Fixed price or hourly?

Fixed price forces clarity. Hourly rewards slowness. Ask how they quote and what happens when scope changes.

2. Who owns the code?

You should own everything: source code, design files, deployed accounts. "No lock-in" should be in writing.

3. How do they communicate?

Weekly demos beat monthly reports. Ask for a demo cadence and a single point of contact.

4. What's the support model?

Bug-fix support post-launch is table stakes. Ask about care plans, response times, and what happens if they disappear.

5. Can they stand in your workshop?

For field-heavy businesses, local presence matters. A partner who sees your process break in real time builds better software.

6. Do they understand your industry?

Generic web devs struggle with offline, BOM, and multi-tenant needs. Ask for relevant case studies.

Red flags

  • No fixed price, only hourly estimates
  • Vague timelines ("it depends")
  • No code ownership clause
  • No post-launch support
  • Can't show a similar project
