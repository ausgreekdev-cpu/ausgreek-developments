Fixed price vs hourly: what actually works for clients

Hourly billing sounds flexible. In practice, it rewards slowness and punishes clarity. Here's why we quote fixed price after a short discovery — and how it protects both sides.

The hourly trap

With hourly billing, the incentive is misaligned: the longer it takes, the more you pay. Scope creep becomes a revenue stream. Clients end up funding exploration, not delivery.

Why fixed price works

  • Predictable budget: you know the cost before we start.
  • Incentive alignment: we profit by being efficient, not by dragging it out.
  • Clear scope: discovery forces both sides to agree on what "done" means.
  • No surprises: 50% deposit, 50% on launch. No hourly invoices.

How we quote

A short discovery call (30 min) → a one-page scope doc → a fixed price. If scope changes, we re-quote. Simple.
