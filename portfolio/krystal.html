Krystal’s Flower Kreations — From Instagram DMs to Online Orders

Challenge: Taking bespoke paper flower orders via Instagram DMs was messy — no gallery, no pricing, no bookings. Orders were missed and quoting took hours.

Solution: Built a mobile-first storefront on Netlify: gallery with filters, product pages, booking form (Netlify Forms), and Stripe-ready checkout. Static hosting keeps it fast and cheap to run.

Stack: HTML/CSS/JS, Netlify Forms, Netlify Hosting. No bloat, no monthly app fees.

Result: Clear pricing and a simple booking flow — customers now self-serve. Owner saves admin time and can add new products via Decap CMS at /admin without code.
