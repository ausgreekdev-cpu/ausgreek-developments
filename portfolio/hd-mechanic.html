HD Mechanic & Scrap — Jobs, Invoices, and Inventory in One Place

Challenge: A WA heavy-duty workshop tracked jobs, invoices, and scrap metal on spreadsheets and paper. Double-entry, lost dockets, and no real-time stock.

Solution: Built a CRM portal: job cards, customer history, invoice generation, inventory and scrap tracking, role-based access. Deployed to Netlify with secure auth and daily backups.

Stack: React + Node, auth, dashboard, Netlify. Designed around the workshop’s actual workflow from day one.

Result: One source of truth — no more spreadsheet merges. Staff can see job status instantly, and the owner can quote faster. ← Back | Build your CRM
