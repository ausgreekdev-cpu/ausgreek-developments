The Problem

Off-the-shelf tools break in the field.

ServiceM8, spreadsheets and paper dockets fail where connectivity drops. Lost data, double-entry, and no real-time visibility.

📶

No connectivity

Mine sites and remote jobs have patchy or no internet. Standard web apps simply stop working.

📋

Lost dockets

Paper job cards get lost, damaged, or entered twice. No audit trail, no accountability.

🔄

Double entry

Field data re-keyed into the office system. Wasted hours, transcription errors, stale stock.
